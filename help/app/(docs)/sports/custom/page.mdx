# Custom Sport

The Custom template is a general-purpose fallback for scoring a sport ScoreHub doesn't have a
named template for yet: 2 periods of 10:00 each, with three plain score buttons.

## Match structure

- Periods: 2 × PERIOD
- Clock: 10:00 countdown per period

## Scoring buttons

- **1, 2, 3** — generic point values; use whichever fits your sport's scoring

## Running the match

Score the match from the buttons above on `/control`. See [Getting started](/getting-started) for
creating the match and finding the control panel link.

<Callout>
  If you score this sport regularly, email
  [hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz) and ask about a dedicated template — new
  sports are config-driven and straightforward for us to add, with proper period labels, clock
  behaviour, and score buttons for your sport. See [Support](/support).
</Callout>
