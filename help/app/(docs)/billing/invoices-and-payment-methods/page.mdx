# Invoices & payment methods

Payment details, invoices, and receipts are managed through Stripe's billing portal, not inside
ScoreHub itself. From **Account → Plan & Billing** (Admins only), the **Manage billing** button
opens your organisation's Stripe customer portal in a new page, where you can:

- Download past invoices and receipts.
- Update or replace your card on file.
- View your current subscription and renewal date.

Changes made in the portal (like updating a card) take effect immediately and don't require
anything further on the ScoreHub side.

<Callout>
  Only an Admin sees the Plan & Billing panel and its **Manage billing** button — Managers,
  Operators, and Viewers don't have access to invoices or payment methods.
</Callout>
