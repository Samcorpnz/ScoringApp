# Upgrading & downgrading

From **Account → Plan & Billing** (Admins only), pick Monthly or Annual billing, then choose a
plan. Checkout happens in place on the Account page — you don't get redirected to a separate
Stripe-hosted page — and your new plan takes effect as soon as payment completes. Billing status
updates automatically within a few seconds; the page shows "Finishing up your upgrade…" while it
waits for confirmation.

## Moving between Pro and Venue

Switching between the two paid plans works the same way as an initial upgrade — pick the new plan
and confirm. Your subscription is updated in place rather than creating a second one.

## Moving to Free

There's no separate "downgrade" button — cancelling your paid plan is how you move back to Free.
See [Cancelling your plan](/billing/cancelling): it takes effect at the end of your current
billing period, not immediately, so you keep paid features until then.
