# Cancelling your plan

From **Account → Plan & Billing** (Admins only), cancelling a paid plan doesn't take effect
immediately — it schedules the subscription to end at the close of your current billing period,
and the page shows the date it cancels on. You keep Pro/Venue features until then, and your
organisation moves to Free automatically once it lapses.

## Changed your mind?

Up until the cancellation date, a **Resume subscription** button appears in place of the cancel
button — using it keeps you on the same plan and interval without having to check out again.

## Cancelling the Graphics Operator add-on

The add-on has its own cancel/resume control, separate from your plan, and works the same way —
see [Graphics Operator add-on](/billing/graphics-addon).

## Closing your account entirely

There's no self-serve "delete organisation" option. Email
[hello@scorehub.co.nz](mailto:hello@scorehub.co.nz) if you want your organisation and its data
removed after cancelling.
