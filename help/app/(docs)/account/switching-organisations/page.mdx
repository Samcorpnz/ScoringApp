# Switching organisations

If you're a member of more than one organisation — say you help run scoring for two different
clubs — an organisation switcher (a dropdown of your organisations) appears in the dashboard
header. It's hidden for accounts that belong to only one organisation, which covers most users.

Picking a different organisation from the switcher re-checks your membership on the server, then
reloads the page in that organisation's context — its matches, its team, and its billing/plan.
Nothing about your login changes; you're switching which organisation you're currently acting as a
member of, not switching accounts.

Each organisation has its own plan, matches, and team, independent of any others you belong to —
see [Billing](/billing) for how plans work per organisation.
