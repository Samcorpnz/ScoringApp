# Connecting a console (Bridge)

Most matches are scored by hand on the [control panel](/getting-started). If your venue has a
physical scoring console, the **ScoreHub Bridge** app reads it and pushes the score to ScoreHub
automatically instead.

## What Bridge is

Bridge is a small app that runs on a laptop at the venue — the one physically connected to the
console — and forwards match state to ScoreHub over the internet. It supports two input sources:

- **Swiss Timing Saturn/Vega consoles** over a serial (RS422) connection.
- **ChampionData**, for sports where scoring comes from a data feed rather than a physical
  console.

Bridge has its own admin UI, served locally at `http://localhost:4002` once it's running, where
you pick the source and configure it.

## Setting it up

1. **Generate a bridge token.** In the control panel, open **Settings** and generate a BRIDGE
   token for your organisation. This is a long-lived credential — treat it like a password and
   don't share it outside your venue team.
2. **Install and start Bridge** on the laptop connected to the console (or running the ChampionData
   feed). Open `http://localhost:4002` in a browser on that same machine.
3. **Enter the bridge token** and the ScoreHub relay URL in the admin UI, and choose your source
   (Saturn/Vega serial port, or ChampionData).
4. **Start the source.** Bridge will begin forwarding match state as soon as the console sends
   data. Set `CD_AUTOSTART=true` in its configuration if you want it to start automatically on
   launch rather than being started from the UI each time.

## Serial console wiring

Saturn/Vega consoles connect over RS422 at 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit (8N1).
Use a laptop with a genuine RS422/RS232 serial port or a known-good USB-to-serial adapter — cheap
adapters are the most common cause of dropped or garbled data.

<Callout>
  A physical console is optional. Everything Bridge can do can also be done by hand from the
  [control panel](/getting-started) — Bridge just saves an operator from tapping the score in
  manually when a console is already running the scoreboard.
</Callout>

Bridge only sends data one way, into ScoreHub — it doesn't read anything back from the app, so
there's nothing to keep in sync manually once it's running.
